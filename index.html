Research Interests

Who gets believed, and who decides what counts.

Education & Policy
How policy and measurement systems decide what counts as learning, with a focus on informal knowledge and children's literacy in Bangladesh, including the place of children's literature in literacy teaching. Approach: conceptual and policy analysis of published research and official documents.
Literary Studies & Media
How young adult fiction ties adolescent credibility to documentary form, and how literary prizes and digital platforms confer value on books for young readers, including Bengali children's literature.

BA Thesis
"Formats of Belief: Documentary Form and Adolescent Credibility in Young Adult Fiction"
A comparative reading of Speak, Dear Evan Hansen, Fangirl, Radio Silence, and Nothing But the Truth, arguing that these novels turn adolescent feeling into evidence through letters, transcripts, and fan-texts, so that credibility rests less on sincerity than on institutional recognizability.
